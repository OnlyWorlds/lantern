## Known gaps (said, not hidden)

- No real phone has run it: WebKit here is Playwright's on Windows. `preparePhoto` on a real iPhone, the home-screen app's separate storage, `persist()` on iOS: the Captain's morning walk.
- Two tabs on one world can both send a row (the loser restores a settled row). One tab is the phone's case.
- Opening a big world file in Playwright's WebKit on Windows: 1,201 elements take 18.6 s, all of it the IndexedDB write (Chromium: 0.1 s). The button says it is opening; whether a real iPhone is that slow is the morning walk's question. A pull of a big world writes the same way.
- A map's picture is not in the worker's precache: it comes from the browser's own cache (media sends a year, immutable), so offline it shows once it has been seen online. A map never opened online says so.
- A shared message (an invite link in it too) reaches Pages in the start URL's query when the worker has no shell cached yet (the first open); with the shell cached nothing leaves the phone. The address is cleaned at once.
- A share arriving while Lantern is already open: the half-typed note is kept as a draft now (S396) and the share lands under it; the photo of a half-made catch is not kept. Whether Android loads the share into the open window is still a real phone's question.
- Pin names (S396) are drawn only when at most 30 named pins are in view, placed so none covers another or a dot; a name with no room is left out (a tap names it). Widths are measured in the label's own font (a font still loading can measure narrow). None on a clustered map.
- Zones answer a tap since S396 (inside an area, or within a finger of a line or an edge; the smallest holding the tap wins; its outline drawn bold, a card with Open). A zone's own name is not drawn on the map.
- The party: Lantern cannot see who the players are (the members list is the owner's route), so it counts the party's Characters, not its players. A Collective of non-player Characters passes the empty-party check while no player sees the reveal.
- A world on this phone keeps a photo as a data URL inside the element; Put it online must upload those first.
